WORD SOUP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Word Soup uses and who may receive it.

Who we are

Word Soup is published by 1-Bit Monochrome. For privacy questions, contact us at 1bit.monochromacy@gmail.com.

Information you give us

You do not need an account to play, and the game does not ask you to enter personal details. If you email us, we and our email service provider receive your email address and the information in your message so we can reply. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to respond and handle follow-up.

Game data on your device

Word Soup stores settings and gameplay progress in Android app storage. This includes language and accessibility preferences, sound and motion settings, recipe progress, scores, earned in-game items, and tutorial progress. You can reset progress in the game’s settings or clear the app’s storage.

Android may include app data in device backups when backup is enabled in your Android settings. Backup and restore are controlled by Android and your Google account. The current release has no player accounts or active cloud-save service.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads. Native ads may appear in the Recipe Book and the pause screen. You can choose to watch a rewarded video to receive an in-game hint. When an ad request is made, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, app and device information, advertising identifier where available, ad activity, and diagnostic data. An IP address may also be used to estimate your general location. This information is used to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Android and Google account settings provide controls for device backups and advertising identifiers.

Children and families

Word Soup is a family-friendly word puzzle and does not ask players, including children, to provide personal information to play. Advertising-related device information may be handled as described above. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Your choices

If Google requires a privacy choices form for your location, you can reopen it in Word Soup under Settings > About & privacy > Manage ad preferences. Resetting progress in the game’s settings removes your journey progress. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings removes local progress and preferences. Uninstalling removes the local copy, but Android may restore backed-up data if a backup is available. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you sent by email. Google handles advertising information and Android backups under its own policies and settings.

Security and updates

Game data is held in Android’s private app storage. Information sent through ads, device backups, or email is handled by the relevant provider under its own security and privacy practices. We may update this policy when the game’s information practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.
